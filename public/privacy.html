GamesTable — Privacy

Last updated: 2026-08-11

GamesTable is a personal tracker for video games. The shortest honest summary: your library never leaves your device, and we could not read it if we wanted to.

What we store

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server that holds user data. Your library lives in your browser’s storage (on the web) or in the app’s own private storage (in the installed app). Deleting your browsing data or the app deletes it — which is why the app keeps offering you an export.

What leaves your device

Searching the catalogue sends your query to the services the catalogue comes from: RAWG and Steam, both through our own proxy. Like any website you visit, they see the request and your IP address. Cover and poster images load from their image hosts the same way.

Catalogue queries pass through our proxy at games-table-bay.vercel.app, which exists only to keep the RAWG API key off your device and to add the CORS headers Steam does not send. It forwards the query, caches popular responses at the edge, and stores nothing about you.

Analytics

The website may report cookieless page views to Vercel Web Analytics — a screen name, never a title, a search term or an identifier. The installed app reports nothing at all.

Backups

An exported backup is an ordinary file created at your request. It goes wherever you send it — your Files, your drive, your email — and never to us.
